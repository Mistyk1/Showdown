# Showdown

**Showdown** is an overall content mod for Balatro. It focuses mostly on new ranks, a new set of cards and fun jokers. It currently adds:
- 7 Ranks
- 97 Jokers
- 8 Decks
- 6 Vouchers
- 7 Tarot Cards
- 3 Spectral Cards
- 2 new sets of cards:
  - 8 Mathematic Cards
  - 10 Logic Cards
- 4 Enhancements
- 8 Stickers
- 27 Tags
- 24 Blinds
- 7 Stakes
- 4 Challenges
- 19 Achievements

The mod is compatible with some other mods, check this out:
- Card Sleeves:
  - 8 Sleeves
- Bunco:
  - 1 Tarot Card
- More Fluff:
  - 7 Rotated Tarot Card (8 with Bunco)
  - 4 Enhancements
- Fusion Jokers:
  - 1 Joker

The mod content is configurable! You can deactivate any category of items or any mod compatibility in the mod config.

You currently need **Steamodded v26.829.0** at least
